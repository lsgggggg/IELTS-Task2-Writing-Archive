写作模板

按题型和话题领域分类的通用写作框架，考前可快速复习和默写

题型1（双边讨论）× 科技

题型1 科技

开头段模板

People hold different views about [话题/技术名称及其影响]. Some argue that [反方观点改述], while others believe that [正方观点改述]. This essay will discuss both perspectives and present my opinion that [自己的立场].
注意：题型1（Discuss both views and give your opinion）必须①讨论双方观点+②亮明自己立场，缺一不可

主体段结构

主体段1（反方观点 + 反驳）：
On the one hand, opponents of [技术/观点] would argue that [反方担忧]. They believe that [反方论据]. This perspective is undoubtedly reasonable to some extent. However, this argument overlooks [反驳角度]. In reality, studies show that [事实依据], which means [结论].

主体段2（正方观点 + 举例）：
On the other hand, no one can deny that [正方核心优势]. For example, [具体举例1]. Furthermore, [具体举例2]. In contrast, those who [反向情况] will significantly [负面后果].

结尾段模板

In conclusion, although [让步——承认潜在问题], I still maintain the view that [最终立场——点题].
技巧：用 "although... I still maintain the view that..." 让步转折结构既亮明立场又显示全面思考

题型3（同意与否）× 生活娱乐

题型3 生活娱乐

开头段模板

It is often argued that [题目核心观点的改写]. From my perspective, I fully agree with this statement, and the following essay will elaborate on the reasons for my view.
注意：题型3要求表达程度（fully/strongly/partially），不能只说 agree/disagree

主体段结构

主体段1（核心论点 + 举例）：
First and foremost, it is undeniable that [核心论点]. To be more specific, [具体解释]. For instance, [举例论证]. [补充说明/与题目关键词衔接].

主体段2（次要论点 + 正反对比）：
Secondly, another compelling reason is that [次要论点]. This is largely because [原因解释]. In contrast, [反面论证].

结尾段模板

In conclusion, I believe [重申立场], even if [让步/补充条件]. Developing this [habit/awareness] will not only help people [好处1], but also offer them [好处2].

题型3（同意与否）× 科技

题型3 科技

开头段模板（三件套——与生活娱乐话题完全一致）

With the boom of [相关科技/行业趋势], significant attention has been drawn to [题目话题核心]. It is often argued that [题目核心观点的改写]. From my perspective, I fully agree with this statement, and the following essay will elaborate on the reasons for my view.
技巧：科技话题首句常用 "With the boom of X, significant attention has been drawn to..." 的背景引入方式；中间 "It is often argued that..." + 结尾 "From my perspective, I fully agree with this statement, and the following essay will elaborate on the reasons for my view" 是题型3 首段三件套固定模板，与 2026-03-21 Saving Money 完全一致，不改动

主体段结构（可选加入让步反驳段）

让步反驳（可选——当立场为 fully agree / fully disagree 时推荐加入，放在主体段 1 开头）：
Admittedly, opponents argue that, given [反方论据的现实背景/前提], [反方主张] rather than [本方立场的核心诉求]. Despite the urgency of these arguments, [主立场的核心命题] is still the ultimate goal of [题目主体].

主体段 1（论点 1 + 论据 1 + 论据 2）：
First and foremost, it is undeniable that [论点 1]. For instance, thanks to [科技工具/现象] now widely used in [领域/场景], [主体] are able to [具体动作], with [具体效益/指标]. Moreover, [主体] can use [科技方法] to [具体任务], thereby [进一步价值].

主体段 2（论点 2 + 论据 1 + 论据 2）：
Secondly, another compelling reason is that [论点 2]. To be more specific, [现象描述]. As a result, [结果陈述]. For instance, [具体例证]. Moreover, [新一代科技] is also reshaping [领域]. For example, [工具 A] can help us [功能 A], while [工具 B] can help us [功能 B]. This greatly improves our efficiency in both work and study.
技巧：Body 1 "First and foremost, it is undeniable that..." 与 Body 2 "Secondly, another compelling reason is that..." 是题型3 的固定模板，与 2026-03-21 Saving Money 完全对齐；举例时连词用 "For instance" / "For example" 引出完整句子——避免 such as / as well as 后接完整从句；举例句式可固化为 "thanks to + N" / "with + N"（伴随状语）/ "X can help us..., while Y can help us..."（while 对比平行）三种

结尾段模板（让步 + 主立场回归）

In conclusion, even though [让步——承认另一面的合理性，如环境/生态/空间探索等非题目核心的科学目标], such as [让步内容的名词短语举例，如 preserving ecosystems or exploring outer space], I still strongly maintain the view that [主立场——重申题目核心命题] remain the primary and ultimate aim of [题目主体].
技巧："In conclusion, even though..., I still strongly maintain the view that..." 是题型3 结尾固定模板；避免 "even though... but also..."（让步-转折错配）——even though 后面接让步内容即可，不能再用 but also；用 "such as + 名词短语" 列举让步内容，不要接完整从句

题型4（利弊类）× 教育

题型4 教育

开头段模板

There is no doubt that [题目背景陈述/现象改写]; therefore, a growing number of [主体：人/国家/机构] have [动作——established / adopted / advocated] [该现象/做法], in order to [该做法的目的]. Having said that, I believe the benefits of [题目主语] outweigh the downsides, especially in terms of [优势点1] and [优势点2].
注意：题型4（Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages）需①改写题目背景 + ②明确表态利大于弊 + ③预告两个优势点，三者缺一不可

主体段结构

主体段1（让步反驳 + 优势1）：
Admittedly, opponents of [题目现象] would argue that [反方担忧], which could make it harder for them to [反方预警后果]. This perspective is undoubtedly reasonable to some extent. However, I believe this problem can be addressed through [反驳解决方案]. In contrast, [题目主体] could give [对象] more opportunities to achieve [优势1]. In other words, [具体阐述], such as [具体举例——a X who wants to..., or a Y who wants to...]. There will be less [负面因素1] and fewer [负面因素2], giving [对象] more opportunities to develop themselves and express their ideas, which will make them more competitive in their future careers.

主体段2（优势2 + 深度阐述）：
Another compelling reason is that [优势2 论点]. To be more specific, [具体场景说明]. [进一步对比/阐述], students are more likely to concentrate on [积极方向] rather than on [消极方向]. As a result, this often leads to [直接结果]. Over time, this supportive environment can shape [长期积极影响].
技巧：题型4 常用"让步反驳+优势"的 3 段式变体——主体段 1 先承认反方意见再给出反驳+优势 1，主体段 2 专注优势 2。用 "rather than" / "As a result" / "Over time" 形成"说明—对比—结果—升华"四层推进

结尾段模板

In conclusion, although [让步——承认另一面], their advantages of offering opportunities for [优势1 总结] and enhancing [优势2 总结] are more significant. Therefore, I still maintain that the advantages of [题目主体] outweigh its drawbacks.
技巧：用 "although..., their advantages of offering... and enhancing... are more significant" 并列动名词保持优势点结构平行；结尾 "I still maintain that..." 与结论立场呼应

题型5（正反利弊）× 教育

题型5 教育

开头段模板

It is true that [题目现象改写]. From my perspective, even though [让步——承认潜在问题], it is a [positive/negative] development because [核心原因1] and [核心原因2].
注意：题型5（Is it positive or negative?）需要①明确表态 positive/negative + ②让步承认另一面 + ③预告两个论点

主体段结构

主体段1（核心论点 + 具体举例）：
To begin with, [核心论点——该现象带来的主要好处/坏处]. To be more specific, [进一步解释]. For example, [具体场景举例]. In contrast, [反面论证——没有该现象时的情况].

主体段2（次要论点 + 举例）：
Moreover, [次要论点——该现象的另一个好处/坏处]. [补充解释]. For instance, [具体例子].

结尾段模板

In conclusion, although [让步——承认问题/另一面], the [opportunity/benefit] it provides for [好处总结] makes it a largely [positive/negative] trend.
技巧：用 "although... the opportunity it provides for... makes it..." 让步+总结结构收束全文，与开头呼应

题型6（问题措施）× 环境

题型6 环境

开头段模板

In recent years, [宏观背景——如 the rapid urbanization of the world] has brought a series of issues to [对象——our planet / our society]. It is true that [题目核心现象改写——如 various natural resources have been consumed at an alarming rate]. In my opinion, there are numerous factors contributing to these problems, and I will also explore potential solutions in the following paragraphs.
注意：题型6（Dangers / Reasons + Solutions）开头段必须①宏观背景 + ②题目现象改写 + ③预告"既分析危害/原因，也探讨对策"双任务。"In my opinion, there are numerous factors contributing to..., and I will also explore potential solutions" 是 Claire 模板中的固化高分句，跨话题通用。

主体段结构

主体段1（危害/原因 + 多利益相关方举例）：
To begin with, when it comes to [问题——如 the dangers of over-consumption of natural resources], numerous stakeholders need to be considered, from [对象A——如 innocent animals] to [对象B——如 related companies and organisations]. Firstly, [第一层危害——如 over-exploitation of resources depletes many animal habitats]. For example, [具体场景——如 cutting trees in the forest for furniture making destroys birds' nests as well as monkeys' homes], ultimately [负面结果——如 reducing biodiversity]. Furthermore, [第二层危害——如 the excessive consumption of fossil fuels causes serious air pollution], resulting in [连锁后果1——如 glacier melting] and intensifying [连锁后果2——如 the greenhouse effect], which also affects [对人类生活的影响].

主体段2（对策 — 官方 + 个人双层次）：
Despite the fact that the issues are severe, there are effective solutions to mitigate them and create a better environment for both humans and [受影响对象]. On the one hand, it is crucial for authorities to implement effective measures, such as [官方对策——如 publishing detailed environmental regulations to limit resource consumption]. On the other hand, individuals should also raise their awareness of [意识领域——如 environmental conservation]. For instance, people can [个人具体行为——如 practise a low-carbon lifestyle], such as [更具体举例——如 driving an electric vehicle to work rather than a gasoline-powered car], in order to [最终目的——如 reduce carbon dioxide emissions].
技巧：①"when it comes to..., numerous stakeholders need to be considered, from A to B" 是高分话题句，一句话罩住整段多维度论证；②主体段2 用"On the one hand (authorities) / On the other hand (individuals)" 双层次对策结构，既全面又清晰，任何"问题措施"题都能套用；③"in order to + 终极目的" 收束句式让对策闭环

结尾段模板

In conclusion, although there are many [dangerous/negative] results of [题目现象], ranging from [危害1] to [危害2], [对策1——官方层面] and [对策2——个人层面] can help significantly alleviate these problems and bring us [美好愿景——如 a better shared home].
技巧：结尾用"ranging from A to B"承接上文危害，再用"X and Y can help significantly alleviate these problems"回扣双层次对策，最后升华为共同愿景，一句话完成危害+对策+升华三件套
